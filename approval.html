Design Language Approval

Approve the direction before we finish the product.

This is not final sign-off on the completed website. It is a controlled approval of the design and content baseline. While an item is under review, change it freely. Once marked YES, it becomes the agreed baseline; reopening it later is treated as a change.

Nothing is transmitted automatically. The buttons prepare or copy your response so you remain in control of what is sent.
