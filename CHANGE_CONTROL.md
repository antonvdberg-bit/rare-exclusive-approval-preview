# Rare & Exclusive — Approval & Change Control

## Purpose
Protect delivery time and maintain a clear agreed baseline while keeping the design-review stage flexible.

## Working rule
**Review → Change → Approve → Lock → Move on**

### Under Review
An item can be changed freely while it remains under review.

### Approved
A written YES / APPROVED from Jan establishes the working baseline for that item.

### Change
If an approved item is reopened later, record it as a change. Assess the impact on:
- scope;
- delivery timing;
- already-completed work;
- external cost, if any;
- commercial fee where the change is material.

## Approval items
1. Logo treatment
2. Overall design language
3. Hero treatment
4. Main headline
5. What Rare & Exclusive is
6. Principles / conduct
7. How We Work
8. Asset classes
9. Private-access / enquiry approach
10. Featured-opportunity treatment
11. Overall direction

## Evidence
Written email approval or the Design Approval Form is the approval record.

## Important
This process does not stop sensible refinement inside an approved direction. It prevents previously settled decisions from being treated as if they had never been approved.
